# Colour to Life

![](/presskit/images/ColourToLife_Main_Image.png)

A virtual reality game where players can breathe life into their environment through the power of their gaze. Placed in complete darkness, they gently illuminate their surroundings, one object at a time. The more they discover the landscape, the more lively it becomes through vibrant colours and relaxing sounds, creating a world of wonder.

## Inspiration
During our discovery period with the [Swiss Paraplegic Group](https://www.paraplegie.ch/fr/), we were inspired by their stories. One patient was able to tell you exactly how many holes are in a ceiling panel, while another expressed that painting is their form of play. We wanted to tap into
these two insights by having observation and creative expression at the forefront of our game.

![](/presskit/images/ColourToLife_Project_Image_2.png)

The simplicity of our game is reminiscent of colourful classics like Where’s Wally and Paint-by-Numbers. But rather than starting with a visible scene, everything begins in darkness. Colouring in larger elements, such as the terrain and mountains, helps with revealing smaller details. Like in Where’s Wally, you’ll need to scan the landscape carefully and pay close attention to your surroundings. The game ends only once the entire scene has been brought to life.

## Credits
This was developed last semester during the Possible Bodies workshop in the Master Media Design program at [HEAD-Genève](https://www.hesge.ch/head/), in collaboration with the Swiss Paraplegic Group. This immersive VR experience is designed to support rehabilitation for people with motor disabilities, using only head movement as the mode of interaction.
